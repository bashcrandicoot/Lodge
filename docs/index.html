From card to calm.

Lodge gets your footage off camera cards safely, checked and neatly named.

Getting started

Default theme, Dark mode. The card line shows how many files are new, and how many were ignored. Coming in the next update This picture’s preset menu is at the top right, and the title is hidden. Lodge 1.0.0 keeps that menu in the center of the toolbar, and the title reads Lodge.

From the card to a folder you trust

Insert a card. Lodge fills the window, left to right: Card, Media, Destination, and the offload bar along the bottom.

SHA-256 on every copy

Lodge writes <name>.lodge-partial, reads it back, and compares the hash with the card. A match becomes the finished file. A mismatch is deleted and reported. The card is only read.

Names you can read later

Templates use {project}, {date}, {camera}, {seq}, and more. The Examples menu in Settings > Naming drops in a ready-made pair, and the main window previews every name before the copy starts.

Only what is new

A ledger remembers each verified file. Insert the same card again and Lodge copies the new ones. The line on the card reads 54 new files · 5 ignored, then 57 copied · 5 ignored when the offload finishes.

Missing copies, called out

If a file in the ledger is gone from the destination, or its size has changed, Media shows an orange count: 3 missing at destination. Re-offload Missing Copies makes a new copy. Existing files stay where they are.

Ready to format

When every file has a verified copy that is still in the destination, the card shows a glowing green Ready to format badge. Format Card… asks first, with Cancel as the default, and keeps exFAT, FAT32, or FAT16.

Short clips, presets, quick dump

Skip videos shorter than runs from Off to 30 s, and starts at 5 s. A preset saves where the footage goes, how folders are named, which camera, short clips, quick dump, and the file rules. You choose it from the preset menu. Lodge remembers the camera for each card and selects it again when that card is inserted. Coming in the next update The preset menu sits at the top right of the window, and the title is hidden. In Lodge 1.0.0 that menu is in the center of the toolbar. Quick dump leaves {project} out so you can just empty the card. The checkbox then reads Quick dump, with an ⓘ that says it skips the project name and dumps the footage into a dated folder. In Lodge 1.0.0 the checkbox reads Quick dump: skip project name.

Drop a folder, or a level

Drop a Finder folder on Destination or on the Layout tree. In Lodge 1.0.0, drag a level to rearrange the folders, or add one from Add a level. Coming in the next update Expand All and Collapse All open the tree, and folder names can sit side by side, such as {project}_{date}, with the separator you pick. A long file list folds to the first name, with + N more under it. An accepted drop clicks on a Force Touch trackpad.

Proxies, sidecars, ignore list

A .lrv file is a proxy, and Lodge does not copy it as video. Settings > Files sends .lrv and .lrf to Proxies/, and .thm, .xml, and .srt to Sidecars/. They keep the clip’s new name. The ignore list is never copied, and Lodge also skips system junk such as recycler and recycled. In Lodge 1.0.0 the other files land in the folder the template makes, and audio recorded with a clip stays next to that clip. Coming in the next update Each extension gets its own folder, such as MP4/, ARW/, and WAV/. .JPG and .JPEG share JPG/. Proxies and sidecars stay separate.

Updates

Lodge 1.0.0 does not check for a new version. Download the next one from Gumroad. Coming in the next update Lodge > Check for Updates… looks now. In Settings > General, under Updates, Automatically check for updates is on, and Automatically download and install updates is off. Lodge checks once a day.

Motion, quietly

The finished checkmark draws itself. The copied count and the size count up. A card glows as it is inserted, rows and tiles lift under the pointer, and the Ready badge glows when it appears. Reduce Motion turns the movement off.

Three steps, then the card can rest

  1. Insert

    Any removable drive under /Volumes shows up in Card, including one that was already mounted. An empty card says Card Is Empty.

  2. Name

    Fill in the project when the template needs it. “Beach Shoot” becomes BeachShoot. Recent projects are one click away. The list shows the finished names.

  3. Offload

    The button says why it is waiting, or Offload 12 Files when it is ready. Afterwards: Show in Finder, Format Card… when the card is ready, Eject Card, and Done.

Default is neutral black

Lodge opens in Dark mode. Default is a neutral black and white window, with no colour wash. Buttons, switches, sliders, selection, progress, and badges use the system accent: system blue, unless you picked another accent in System Settings.

  • Aurora, Ocean, Twilight, Ember, and Forest are optional colour themes. Each tints the window wash, the Offload button, the progress bar, the glowing border of the step that needs you, and the dots that travel between steps.
  • Light, Dark, or System is a switch in Settings > Appearance. Dark is the setting Lodge starts with.
  • Increase Contrast and Reduce Transparency turn every theme to solid colours.

Install Lodge 1.0.0

Lodge is a universal app for Apple silicon and Intel Macs. It requires macOS 14 or later. Download Lodge from Gumroad, then open the disk image and drag Lodge to Applications.

macOS 15 Sequoia and later

  1. Open Lodge.
  2. Click Done on the warning.
  3. Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  4. Click Open Anyway next to Lodge, and confirm.

macOS 14 Sonoma

  1. Right-click or Control-click Lodge.
  2. Choose Open.
  3. Choose Open again.

Lodge is not notarized, which is why macOS asks.

The first time Lodge reads a card, allow access to files on a removable volume. Building from source is in the contribution guide. The user guide, FAQ, and changelog live in the repository.
