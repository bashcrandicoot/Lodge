# Changelog

## 1.0

_Oct 31, 2026_

Home base for your media. [3-day trial, then $10 once.](https://bashfra.me/lodge-dmg)

- **Naming templates** with tokens, a live preview and continued numbering.
- A folder for each file type (MP4, WAV, ARW…) inside the last folder. JPG and JPEG share one.
- **Verified copies.** Each file is written under a temporary name, read back and checked with SHA-256, then renamed. Nothing is overwritten.
- Notices camera cards and removable drives with media as you insert them.
- A Card, Media, Destination window with an offload bar along the bottom.
- Copies only new files. An offload ledger remembers what was already copied.
- Sidecars keep their clip’s name. Proxies go into Proxies/ and sidecars into Sidecars/, with an editable extension and ignore list.
- **Presets** for destination, naming, camera, short clips, Quick dump, and file rules.
- Skip short videos: a slider from Off to 30 s, and a stepper up to 60 s.
- Favorite destinations with available space, or drop a Finder folder.
- Re-offload missing files or the whole card.
- Safe card formatting, with a **Ready to format** badge.
- Thumbnails, list and grid view, and Quick Look.
- Recent Offloads with Show in Finder.
- Themes: default dark, Aurora, Ocean, Twilight, Ember, and Forest. Small animations that respect Reduce motion.
- **Menu bar mode** that watches for cards and asks to import them, as a notification or a floating panel.
- **First-launch setup.** A short guide to your first preset.
- **Date filter.** Offload Any date, Today, the Last 7 days or a custom range.
- Disk images (.dmg) on a card are ignored.
- **Check for Updates**, with automatic checks you can turn off in Settings > General.
- **Tip Jar…** is the in-app tip. bashframes contact details are in About Lodge and the Help menu.
- The Lodge Cabin app icon.
- Available space shows correctly on non-APFS external drives (exFAT, HFS+).
- Read-only drives, such as NTFS, get a clear message.
- **Help > Report a Bug…** emails bashframes@gmail.com with the log attached, and Lodge offers to send a crash report after an unexpected quit.
- Show in Finder opens one window on the parent folder.
- About Lodge lists the open-source acknowledgements.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Universal app for Apple silicon and Intel Macs. Lodge isn't notarized, so the first time, right-click Lodge and choose Open, or go to System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.
