## Lodge 1.0

*Home base for your media.* Your folders, your names, every card. Every copy is checked with SHA-256, so your footage comes off the card safely.

- **Notices cards as you insert them.** Any removable drive with media on it, including cards that were already in when Lodge opened.
- **A Card → Media → Destination window**, read left to right, with the offload bar along the bottom.
- **Copies only what's new.** A ledger remembers every offloaded file, so inserting the same card again copies only new files.
- **Verified copies.** Each file is read back from disk and checked with SHA-256 before it gets its final name. Nothing is ever overwritten.
- **Naming templates** with tokens (`{project}`, `{camera}`, `{date}`, `{time}`, `{type}`, `{card}`, `{original}`, `{seq}`, `{ext}` and more), template presets and a live preview of every final name.
- **Sidecars and proxies sorted.** Sidecars keep their clip's name; proxies go into `Proxies/` and sidecars into `Sidecars/`.
- **Presets.** Save a whole setup and switch to it from the toolbar.
- **Quick dump.** Skip the project name and offload a card right away.
- **Skip short videos.** Accidental takes under a few seconds stay on the card.
- **Favorite destinations** with their free space, one click apart.
- **Re-offload** missing copies or the whole card, without overwriting.
- **Format a card safely.** A **Ready to format** badge appears once every file has a verified copy.
- **Thumbnails, list view and Quick Look.**
- **Recent Offloads** with **Show in Finder**, which opens one window on the folder the copies share.
- **Themes.**
- **Menu bar mode.** Keep Lodge in the menu bar to watch for cards.
- **First-launch setup** that walks you through your first preset.
- **A folder for each file type** inside the last folder.
- **Choose which days to offload:** Any date, Today, Last 7 days or a custom range.
- **Correct free space on external drives** that aren't APFS, and a clear message when a destination drive is read-only.
- **Help ▸ Report a Bug…** with Lodge's log attached, and a crash-report prompt on the next launch.
- **Check for Updates** (automatic checks can be turned off in Settings).
- **Tip Jar…** in the Lodge and Help menus, for optional tips.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Universal: runs natively on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

## First launch

Lodge isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS warns you the first time you open it. You only have to do this once:

- **macOS 15 Sequoia and later:**
  1. Open Lodge and click **Done** on the warning.
  2. Go to **System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security**.
  3. Scroll down, click **Open Anyway** next to Lodge, and confirm.
- **macOS 14 Sonoma:** right-click (or Control-click) Lodge in Applications, choose **Open**, then click **Open** in the dialog.

The first time Lodge reads a card, macOS may ask whether *"Lodge would like to access files on a removable volume"*. Click **Allow**.
